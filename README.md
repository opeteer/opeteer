### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Software & Cloud-Native Engineer** passionate about building robust, distributed, and high-performance applications. My work spans across **Kubernetes & DevOps automation**, **systems engineering in Go & Python**, **native Android/cross-platform mobile apps**, and **full-stack web platforms**.

* 🔭 **Currently Building**: Ad-hoc disaster relief energy & communication systems for **FIT COMPETITION 2026** ([`PANDORA`](https://github.com/opeteer/PANDORA)).
* ⚡ **Systems & High-Performance**: Engineering custom cloud storage engines ([`hornetzDrive`](https://github.com/opeteer/hornetzDrive)) and modular frameworks ([`ztatic-go-framework`](https://github.com/opeteer/ztatic-go-framework)) in **Golang**.
* ☁️ **Cloud & Infrastructure**: Architecting Kubernetes clusters ([`infra-for-kube`](https://github.com/opeteer/infra-for-kube)), GitOps IDP pipelines, and network automation with **Tailscale & Docker**.
* 📱 **Mobile & Platforms**: Developing intuitive native Android apps with **Kotlin** and scalable multi-role platforms with **React Native / PHP**.
* 🎯 **Core Philosophy**: Clean architecture, automated infrastructure, and performance-first design.
